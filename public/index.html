Choose the kit that fits where you want to mount it

Nozzle Nest® is available in three mounting options. All three bracket types are available in black or pink.

NN00 Kit

Flat mounted

Standard flat-mounted Nozzle Nest for direct fixing to a wall, cupboard side, or panel.
Simply uses two screws, provided.

NN45 Kit

45° angled mount

Includes angled mounting brackets to tilt the Nozzle Nest forward for easier access.
Best for holding larger brushes.

NN90 Kit

90° stand-off mount

Uses stand-off brackets to position Nozzle Nest upright, saving the most space. Ideal for van interiors and small cupboards.

All options come with the relevant screws, covers and brackets.

Full fitting instructions are included with every kit.

Compatibility

Designed specifically to be compatible with:

  • Henry®
  • Hetty
  • James

Not compatible with:

  • Dyson
  • Shark
  • Hoover
  • Other non-Numatic vacuum systems

If you’re unsure which fittings you have, please check before ordering a Nozzle Nest as it is intentionally designed for Numatic systems only.
